# Background

- He was intelligent and kind
    - Spotted connections between events occurring in the kingdom that seemed to lead to a bigger conspiracy, but he could not understand it fully
        - Did not have sufficient access to information to deduce fully
    - Felt the great class imbalance and social structure to be wrong
        - Wishes to correct it but have no idea how
    - He felt as if he was drowning. He understood enough of the world to know how little he knows and how much he doesn’t understand. It was suffocating.
- These thoughts drowned him, until his sister was born
- His sister disappeared like all the other kids after failing the blood trial. He could not find out where she went
- Gave water to a town that supports house Aphelion, which house Kendra has been targeting for a long time to make the town support them instead, and made the Lords extremely angry so they exiled him to Nirvana
- He joined the Scouts to help children get by
- He found out his sister, Mary, is in lower floors of the cave so he asked his friends in the Scouts to help him rescue her
- They reached her, but she was already mostly possessed by a devil. It was too late to save her.
- Gavin did not have the heart to kill his sister, and the devil possessing her killed most of his friends who went with him
- He escaped. Filled with guilt. He now bounce from bounty to bounty, just enough to get by.
- He visits the brothel often to relieve stress and became addicted to sex, but the past never stop haunting him.
- He also puts on a front to be confident, but the trauma had destroyed his self-esteem. Drowning in sorrow.

# Goals and Motives

## Kill Mary

- To release her from the devil’s possession

## Abolish the corrupt system

- Same as Mirasol, he had suffered, and had witnessed countless people suffer under the Nirvana system, he wants to abolish it so no on has to suffer like they did

## Find what the king is looking for before he does

- With the help of other people who had looked too deep into the kingdom’s motives, he deduced that the king is probably looking for something in Evernight. He wants to find it no matter how many people suffer. Hence, Gavin wants to find it before he does, then decide what to do with it. Perhaps use it as leverage to dethrone him or to change the laws.
- Helps him understand more of the world as well

## Help Sophie with her bucket list

- He sympathizes with Sophie a lot and cares deeply about her. He wants to help her do all the things she wanted.
- By the time she can tick everything off, the world should be a better place

# Conflict

- Being a bounty hunter naturally comes with enemies
- Enemies of Mirasol and Sophie, are also his enemies
- His own fears and worries getting in the way to achieve his goals
- The guild killed his friend to regulate Nirvana’s population, he was out for revenge
    - He understood why it was necessary, but could not accept it
    - He wants to kill the council, but it would set a bad example for Sophie. He cannot have her kill them either
- He felt powerless against the corrupt system. He wants changes, but doesn’t want to want changes.
- Knows that Mirasol needed to sacrifice herself, but doesn’t want her to, nor does he want to tell her.

# Revelation

- He was afraid of responsibilities due to the fear of failure, but the people around him will accept him despite of failures. No one should be expected to kill their loved ones without a thought.
    - Mirasol showed him that it was ok if he failed. She would not blame him. What matters is they tried.
- He did not fall into the deepness. The moment he was born he has been in it. What he learned only made him realize this fact. Realize how small he is compared to the world, and how little he can do to change anything.
- However, this means that everyone else is in the deepness as well. Of course, Mirasol and Sophie would be too.
- He did not know where he is in the deepness. But he knows he was next to Mirasol and Sophie. He can feel their touches. That is all he needed.
- That is what he needs to do what he needs to do. Hold their hands tight. And never let go.
- He did not feel lost anymore, and his soul became much richer.
