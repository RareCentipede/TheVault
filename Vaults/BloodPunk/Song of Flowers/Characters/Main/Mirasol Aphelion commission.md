# Background
- Born into the most power noble family in Zinariya. She was bred to inherit a special gene that makes her the perfect sacrifice for the sun
- Nobles and children did all they could to please her to get close to her, to receive blessings after she's sacrificed. She doesn't know about the sacrificial ritual but she could tell they weren't genuine, which annoyed her a lot and is the source of her short temper.
	- She also felt that her father only used her as a tool to get favours or information from other families.
- During a scavenging trip outside of the kingdom walls, she helped Patricia find her late father's emblem. From there she became friends with several others too. The first group of genuine friends she made. They all cared about each other a lot.
- Patricia was performing poorly in physical exams. And after finding out what the house did to failed nobles, she panicked and forced her friend to train very hard so she could pass the test.
	- However, the blood trial is mostly testing the genetics, so it is impossible train for.
	- Mirasol was so desperate for connections that she was too scared to lose any friend.
- The traditionalists were plotting. They want don't want to sacrifice Mirasol as that would make the sun stay up forever which would remove the need for Aphelion to exist.
	- The other houses also became part of this faction, as the sun would rid Ever-night of corruption, and their influences would diminish in the kingdom.
- Knowing this, Mirasol's father and the other reformists plotted to have Mirasol cause a terrible accident. He would then plead to the king to lock her up and heavily guarded instead of executing her, as she would be sacrificed for the good of all anyways.
- Her father then told her about an experimental crystal locked away in the basement that could improve one's genetics. Implying that she could use it to help Patricia past the blood trial.
- Mirasol sneaked into the basement, which was miraculously unlocked, to fetch the crystal.
	- However, he friends hurried up behind her. They were told she was doing something dangerous so they came to check on her.
		- Mirasol's uncle had a bug planted among the reformists who relayed their plan to the traditionalists. Her uncle then told her friends about her going to the basement.
	- She assured them everything is fine and reached for the crystal. It cut her finger and as her blood came into contact with the crystal fire blaze out, setting the whole basement aflame.
- It scorched her left eye and burned all her friends to crisp.
- She was sentenced to death for "breaking into the basement" and killing noble children with very high potentials, according to the accusations from a higher up of the house.
	- A lot of witnesses and evidence was forged
	- They also argued that Mirasol is no longer pure after committing such a horrible crime. She does not deserve to be sacrificed to the sun.
- After a lot of pleading, the king allowed the execution to be delayed.
- Her father locked away parts of her memory to ease her pain and manipulated the house to exile Mirasol instead. Sending her to an underground mining facility. Where the story begins.
- The leaders of the reformists decided to break Mirasol out and deliver her directly to the sacrificial furnace.
