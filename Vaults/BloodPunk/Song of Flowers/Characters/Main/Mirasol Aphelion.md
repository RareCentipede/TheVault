# Background
- Father was an Ember Lorder of house Aphelion
- After she was chosen as a Sun Maiden people became cold to her
    - Sun maidens are known to live very short lives, so people don’t bother connecting with her
    - This made her anxious, but a few friends stuck by, including a boy that fancied her
- One of her younger friends was performing poorly in pre-tests and was worried. So Mirasol gathered her friends to look for ways to help her
    - She got a tip from her uncle, which was to use the blood stone locked in the basement, not knowing it was an experiment stone and was extremely unstable
    - She got her friends to fetch the stone together, and sliced her finger on the sharp edge of the stone, which triggered her bloodline art and burned them to crisp
- This memory was so traumatic that Lord ember asked the Keyper to seal this memory away. There was some residual of Jake’s soul in Mirasol so she used it to guard her memories.
- People started avoiding Mirasol even more. Lord ember also was too pre-occupied to take care of her properly. She often ran out and got into fights in hopes of her father coming and caring for her. She grew temperamental
    - One time she ran so far that she reached a rural village and met Janet. She did not know much about nobles and became friends with her. Her father had died in battle.
        - “What I’m trying to say is, you’re not alone. I’m just as depressed as you are. If you need someone to share the pain with, please come to me.”
        - “And promise you’ll come back! You gotta teach me boxing!”
- This never happened until the day they departed. Lord ember sensed the traditionalists in the house are planning to take her life so he caused a huge disaster and took her away. But he could only run so far, so he asked someone to sneak her into Nirvana, in hopes she’d live for a bit longer, while he distracted the search parties.
