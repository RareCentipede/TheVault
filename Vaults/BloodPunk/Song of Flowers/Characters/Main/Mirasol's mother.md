Men and women of Aphelion were chosen at random by Apollo to give birth to his heirs, their sacrifice are much stronger than regular Aphelion's, and when 9 have been sacrificed Apollo can be reborn and bring eternal sunlight to the world.

Mirasol's mother already has a great family of two kids and a loving husband. When she was marked she attempted to run away with her family but was caught, and her family all died during the conflict. Her resentment for the house started then.

After giving birth to Mirasol, she resented her with all her heart. Mirasol's uncle, part of the Veil Wardens, schemed with her to sabotage the heir of Apollo's sacrificial ritual. He marked runes of resentment on her body, and had her take her own life in front of Mirasol. The tanha infused with her to cause her endless pain.

For a while, resentment would disrupt her daily life, but then became quiet for a long time, waiting for the perfect moment to inflict the most amount of suffering onto Mirasol. When her father sent her to Nirvana, he also asked an Ice Lord to freeze all memories of Mirasol from the nobles of Zinariya, so no one would even think to search for her.

Her mother let her build a great close relationship with Gavin and Sophie, and others at Nirvana, and faked being captured by Jake. Then at the right moment, broke free and shattered the ice prison blocking memories of Mirasol. And the Veil Wardens came, and took everything from Mirasol.
