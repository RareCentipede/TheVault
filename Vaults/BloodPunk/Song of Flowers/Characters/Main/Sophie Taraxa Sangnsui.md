# Background

- She was part of the Taraxa project
    - Attempt to create homunculi from blood stones to create a powerful army
- Wallace, the lead researcher of the project, wanted to give his infertile wife a child, and Sophie was chosen
    - He did left out some runes when creating her blood stone, so she did not become an obedient weapon
- However, his wife had disappeared without a trace
- He raised Sophie in secrecy by himself for a while, until the house found out and demanded him to dispose her immediately
- He pleaded to let her live by having her do dirty jobs like the other Taraxas
    - At first it only involved killing invading demons, but the demon lords sent out people, too. While regular soldiers stepped back and hesitated, the Taraxa soldiers eliminated the enemies without a second thought
    - Sophie could not do the same. It quickly weighed on her. But she did not want her father to be in trouble, so he forced herself to commit murder.
- Her simple joy was to share food with her father and Cadmus
- Wallace soon found out what the generals had made her do horrible things and realized how foolish he was
- He found out the plans the lords had for Taraxas with the core of Chaos. He copied the signature rune into Sophie’s core and destroyed the original rune
    - Kept it in Sophie in hopes she can use it to protect herself
- He asked Cadmus to take her away to Nirvana, while he thought of ways to find his wife Margret.
    - Before they departed, he asked Cadmus to be her godfather and give her a proper name.
- When arrived at Nirvana, Cadmus told her to not stay too close to him, as he might become a target for people and hence she would be involved
- She lived a simple life in Nirvana day to day.
