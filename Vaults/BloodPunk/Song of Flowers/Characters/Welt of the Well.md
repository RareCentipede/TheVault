#character
_Pain was all he felt. But the first thing he received, was kindness_.

# Overview
- Welt helped defending humans against the night, killing demons and other monsters one after the other.
- After getting severely injured during a battle, he found that he was made of the same things demons were made of. He was the same as them.
- He became conflicted. It made no sense to him why he would continue to destory himself. And began questioning who he was, what his purpose was. Why does he walk the earth. Why was he born.
- These questions bothered him day and night. Eventually driving him to leave the empire he created to pursue answers.

# Story
The thing awoke in the well. It knew not where it was, or what it was. It was in complete darkness.

Images of people flashed in its mind. Thousands, millions, billions of faces. They cried, they yelled, they screamed. In agony, in sorrow, in anger. It screamed in returned as it felt cold chilling to its bones, heat melting its flesh, toxins burning its insides. Swords, knives, bullets tore its body apart. Every shape of pain and every color of misery were forced upon it again and again and again as it let out the most heart wrenching cry.

“MAKE IT STOP!” it cried.

...

The darkness did not respond.


It knew not how long it cried for. The pain did not go away.


The flashing images slowed down and it was able to see and it was shown.


A face of a man twisted in anger and hatred rammed a sword through its chest, on top of a mountain of faceless corpses.

This was pain.

A dying woman laid on a bed. It held her hand as her pulse weakened, and weakened, and slowed to a halt.

This was sadness.

It slouched on a chair in a dim lit room surrounded by empty and spilled bottles of alcohol. It swayed in the chair swig after swig until the bottle can no longer give while the noose dangled above its head.

This was misery.

Its head was stomped on by a foot as it watched a woman struggled in the arms of a group of burly men, screaming in horror and pleads. It gritted its teeth and could do nothing but scream.

This was anger.

Flames engulfed the pit. Hundreds around it screamed in pain along with it as their flesh melted and the shadowed figures at the top laughed and mocked.

This was despair.

This was horror.

This was loneliness.

This was sorrow.

This was anguish.

This was wrath.

This was jealousy.

This was hunger.

This was...

This was...

This...

...

...

...

“Is someone in there?" a gentle voice said. It echoed from far away.
At that instant all the horrors and pain it was enduring went away.

...

"You've been screaming. Are you alright?" The same voice said.


A circle of light. And a small black dot.


"Hello?"


"...hey" the thing in the well said, weakly. "who are you?"
