#Theme 
# How do we find our purpose
- Sometimes given to us at birth
	- Can either accept or reject
	- Exploitation
	- Inheritance
		- Eidolon evolution or mutation
- We can also find our own purpose, guided by memories and emotions
	- Can cause conflict with the purpose we were given
	- Finding a purpose itself is a difficulty

# Betrayal
- We can be betrayed by our perceived purpose
	- Due to circumstances
	- Life long of work leading nowhere, or even pain

# Fulfilling our purpose
- Pursuing at all cost
	- Can be destructive and lonely
	- Obsession leads to [[Tanha and Mara#Tanha]]
- A journey carried by emotions
- Obsessing over our perceived purpose can make us miss everything else life has to offer

# What comes first
- Do we define our purpose, or does our purpose define us?
- Defining self
	- Through producing things using all our knowledge and experiences
	- By others and the environment

# Acceptance
- True happiness comes from accepting and even celebrating one's identity

# Ties to Eidolon
- Strong sense of purpose -> strong Eidolon
- Drive and Resolve
	- Strength to go on
	- Determination to do what is needed
