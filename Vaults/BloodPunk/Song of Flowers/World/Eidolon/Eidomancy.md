#concept 
The activation, manifestination, and resolution of Eidolons.

# Philosophy
- Eidomancers believe the end point of Eidomancy is the discovery and definition of self.
- Eidolons are closely intertwined with one's identity. Strong definition of self leads to stronger and more unique Eidolons

# Restriction of practice
- [[Ministry of Eidology]] prohibits the practice of Eidomancy among commoners.
- Inherited Eidolons are more stable, as they are expressed through tangible and known elements.
	- Fire, water/ice, blood.
- The nature of Eidolons leads to extremely unpredictable abilities, making it difficult to control the populace.
	- Can also lead to catastrophies.
- Eidomancers believe that non-Eidomancers do not possess 'self', and are therefore the lesser people, even though the ministry of Eidology prohibits the practice of Eidomancy among commoners.
- In rare cases the Ministry recognizes useful Eidomancers among the commoners, and invite them to special defense forces for the Empire.
- [[Arson (Solaire) Aphelion]]'s agency seeks out such people and either recommend them to the Minstry or hires them to deal with Eidolon-related incidents in the Empire.

# Cost
- No power comes for free.
- For Eidomancers, they spend their blood and let the Tanha nibble on their soul.
- Stronger and more complex Eidolons require more blood and soul to use.
- Souls can grow back over time as long as there is still some left.
- Damage to the soul is reflected on one's physical body.

# Group resonance
- Many people that share similar desires and goals can join their blood together to manifest extremely strong Eidolons.
