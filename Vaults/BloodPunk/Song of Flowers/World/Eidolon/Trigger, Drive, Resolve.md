# Three components of Eidolon

## Trigger
[[Memories]] and [[Emotions]]
- Activates the Eidolon
	- Drawing from strong memories and emotions
	- Sets the basis of the Eidolon

## Drive
[[Memories]] and [[Purpose]]
- Sustains the Eidolon
	- Keeps the effect going as long as the user is willing
	- Sets the duration and strength of the Eidolon

## Resolve
[[Purpose]] and [[Emotions]]
- Manifests the Eidolon
	- Shapes the effect and potentially the physical form of the Eidolon
	- Sets how the intended end result is reached, and ends the effect.

Inbalance of the three will cause Tanha overflow, and can lead to Mara-born.
