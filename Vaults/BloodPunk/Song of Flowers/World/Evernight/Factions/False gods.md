#faction 
# Overview
- Beings that received power from carcasses of dead gods.
	- Some gods are faintly alive. Manipulating their followers to do their biding, which is usually to kill demons or invade Zinariya.
- These dead [[Gods]] are the same as devils in essence. Only difference is that they ascended from human to godhood in ancient times, but were slain during Ragnarok.

# Sub-factions
## Messiah
- Some resonates with the dead gods' bodies, like one does with devils, and receive their powers.
	- An offering or contract must be formed
- These people were mistaken as real gods by common people.
- Religious states. The resonators were worshipped and protect the people from the darkness.
- As such, the carcass of the dead god is also worshipped and seen as holy, and must not be tempered with.
## Crusaders
- It is possible to eat the dead gods' flesh to receive their powers.
- However, most mortals cannot withstand the density and complexity of runes in the gods' flesh
- One must build up to it over many years by drinking small amounts of the gods' blood and study their ways.
- Even then, most cannot withstand eating their flesh.
- Those that survive the trial become Crusaders. An army of powerful beings sworn to protect their people.
- The gods' carcasses are seen as divine. These states are also religious.
- Memories are carried in flesh too. Those that consumed the gods' flesh and survived will see visions of their past, and they will start to behave like them as well.
## Heretics
- People who repurpose the carcasses into weapons and armor to fight the darkness.
	- Same way demons' bodies can be remade into weapons.
- Contains the runes and powers of the god.
	- Designed to best utilize a specific rune ability, or a set of rune abilities.

These three groups are constantly at conflict. The Messiah states see the gods' carcass as divine and must not be tempered with. The Crusaders believe it is the gods' wishes to bless them with their divine blood and flesh. Hence, refusing to eat them is rejecting the gods' gifts. While the Heretics believe the gods have failed them, and is why Everynight is created which is full of monsters and demons. Thus, they made do as they pelase with the gods bodies as punishment.
