#deity 
# Overview
- People that received power from [[Tharisha-The Blessed One]].
- Before Evernight and Zinariya came to be, the whole world bathed in sunlight's warmth. But due to the growth of Rhyze, devils, demons, and other corrupted creatures came to be, and threatened the lives of common folks.
- Tharisha rose to power and granted many people powers equivalent, and sometimes stronger, than the threats.
- However, after all the corruption has seemingly been destoryed, these people continued to hunger for power.
- They abused their gifts, bringing forth [The age of Gods]

# The age of Gods
- The gifted humans absued their powers and relentlessly sought after ways to fulfill their desires.
- Technology advanced immensly. Countless cities rose to the sky. The capital of the Gods was built. A giant floating palace, which they shamelessly named 'Eden'.
- The gifted ones obtained more and more power by fulfilling their desires. Their bodies began to shift and morph, until they were distorted beyond recognition.
- They named themselves Gods. Their physical body bore no resemblance to their former selves, or any creatures known to man. They became a higher life form.
- The once righteous and humble heroes now posess power beyond comprehension and sat on their shiny thrones playing gods, controlling all of humanity. It was peaceful, but not everyone is fond of them.
- As they grew more powerful, they also grew even more hungry for power. Their ascended minds could not stand boredem, and they have pleased themselves to all the enjoyment humankind could offer, and more. Often at the humans' expense. So they directed their attention to the very things that built up the world.
- They tried to take control of nature itself.
- Apollo took the sun. But it was too powerful, so he split it into nine parts, and gave the other eight to his brothers. They agreed to take turns to provide mankind with sunlight, and would refrain from doing so should humans upset them.
- Tharisha saw the danger of the Gods actions, and warned them to take their powers away if they continue to abuse them.
- They did not listen. And saw her as a threat, and hunted her down to absorb her powers, so they will be above all.
- And thus began [Ragnarok]

# Ragnarok
- The origin of Tharisha's power was  [[Welt of the Well]]
- Seeing the cruelty the gods have committed on Tharisha, all the painful memories of betrayals flooded back to his mind.
- After making her last wish, Welt erupted out of the well, and waged war against the gods.
- He called forth an unfathomable quantity of Rhyze and created monsters based on humans' fears and horrible desires. To defeat the gods using what they feared the most.
- He won. He killed every last god and anyone that bore any resemblance. But this caused irreversible damage on the world. His wrath went too far. Night and day are now separated. Devils and demons roamed the lands once more.
- After killing the gods, he was praised by the humans as their savior, for they had suffered the gods' sadistic plays for far too long.
- Welt led the humans for a while, and created the empire of Zinariya, the land of the Sun. Eventually, he decided to leave, and opened a library in the Evernight.
