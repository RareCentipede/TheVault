# Tanha
- Abstract, intangible beings that “embody” the concept in their name
- They form when an abundant of Rhyze gather and combine, creating extremely complex rune combinations, such that it can resonate with people who share similar emotions/desires that is carried in the Rhyze.
- Views the world through people that share the concept
	- Intangible because the Rhyze lump is not technically their body. They have no body. No concious. The lump simply makes the devil's effect noticeable.
## Contracts
- Can interact with those that strongly possess the concept (The seven sins, all types of fears, vengeance, darkness etc)
    - Offer a binding contact to the individual
    - Power at a price
    - Tanha are free to make the contract however unfair as they want
        - They also must stick to it, otherwise they would be wiped from existence
- Tanha are not concious like we are. They act on stimulation and resonance, like jellyfishes.
- Tanha can possess the body of those that break their contract, or as a part of the terms in it, and become Mara

# Mara
- Tangible beings that enbody the concept in their name
- Tanha that gained enough power to create a physical form for themselves
- Tanha that possessed a contract breaker’s body
    - Can also possess based on the contract’s terms
- Their flesh contains powerful runes
    - Can be processed into weapons and armor
- Their cores are extremely valuable
    - Contains life information, it became a living, concious being through Rhyze.
    - Key to reviving the demon
## The birth of a Demon
- When a demon is born, runes are transferred from the original Rhyze lump to the new body, and the Rhyze loses all its runes and become useless rocks.
- Hence, when the demon is destroyed, it does not return to be a devil. Its original 'self' no longer exists.
- However, if the core is still in tact, the demon can be revived. And even if the core is broken, if it can be put back together, the demon can also be revived.
