# 5 Major forces

## Guild of Rumbling Rose

The guild provides alternative ways to earn money
### Bounties
- Dangerous creatures roam in the depths of the mines
- They pose as threats to miners
- They also sometimes disrupt the caves, blocking sources of rhyze
- The guild pays the parties that eliminates the bounty targets
    - Bounty takers must provide proof that they indeed killed the target
    - Often done by providing the core or body parts of the monster
    - The core can be reprocessed either into unmarked blood stones or into equipment, if it has significant rune signature
    - Body parts are sold to butchers to process into edible meat, or to researchers
- When new threats are reported, the guild collects information about the target, their features and abilities, then contacts The Board who will publish the target to the outside world, where interested individuals (people in power, the rich, nobility, and researchers) can place bids on the target
- Parts of the money goes to The Board, the guild takes a small service fee, and the rest goes to the party that eliminated the bounty target

### Hidden bounties

- An underground group accepts bounties on humans in Nirvana
- No rules on who, as long as one dares to place the bounty and another dares to take it

### Businesses

- Citizens can submit business proposals which will be reviewed by the guild council and The Board
- Once reviewed and sufficient funds are provided the business can be formed
- Infrastructure will be built with the help of The Board
- The guild only receives an initial service fee
- The Board takes a cut of the earnings
- Some people would do anything to avoid working in the mines

### Death Roulette

- Nirvana is flourishing and the population is booming
- Since resources are still very limited, Reina has decided to constrain the population to prevent overpopulation
- When the population exceeds a limit calculated based on current available resources, random people are chosen to be killed, in order to keep the population count in check
- The guild has began to offer exclusion from the roulette in exchange for money
- Sophie is one of the people in charge to the killing

## Self-Policing Unit (SPU)

The SPU is independent from the enforcers, who work for the Board. The SPU focuses on the safety of the people of Nirvana, while the enforcers only care about the kingdom’s profit and to make sure no one leaves Nirvana.

The police.

## Flower Street

The Flower Street is a red light district

### The Nightingale

- Those that choose to work at the Flower Street are under the protection of the Nightingale
    - A devil contractor who will hunt down those that commit sexual assault on people under its wings
- Minors can work as servants, the Nightingale will protect them from sexual intercourse until they have matured
- An alternative to those that are too weak for the mines, and do not have the means to start a business
- The job market is extremely competitive, few people have the luxury to work a normal job

## The Scouts

An organization for Nirvana’s children, to provide them with a positive environment to grow and to prepare them to be independent underground one day. The president is a former lord of house Kendra.

- Pays poorly, not a lot of good hearted people to oversee the kids
- Not all children can join due to understaff

## The Board

Oversees the operation of Nirvana, makes sure there is stable output of blood crystals and other materials and input of resources. They control the enforcers.

### Enforcers

- To control the citizens and protect the Board
- Regulates the flow of materials and the inflow of people
- Led by Cadent the Executioner, a legend in house Sanguis
- Eliminate evil contractors
