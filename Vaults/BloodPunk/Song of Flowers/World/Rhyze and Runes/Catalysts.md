#concept 
Processed [[Rhyze]] in cubic crystal form, always unmarked, meaning it contain no runes.
- Can be used with a [[Driver]] to activate runes
	- Uses blood as fuel
	- Bloodline/inherited runes
	- Mutated runes from emotional fluctuation and unquenchable desires
- Marking runes into Catalysts turns them into [[Eidolith]]
- Nothing would happen if a person with no significant runes try to use a catalyst.
- Abilities from cores cannot be affected by music
- Abilites from signature runes may be affected by music
- For living being with signature runes, catalysts act as the trigger medium
- For living being without signature runes, their body is the trigger medium
