# Man-made Eidolith
[[Catalysts]] marked with stable rune programs
- Triggered by unmarked blood
- Can be made extremely small and be arranged in a circuit pattern
- Used for weapons and machinery
	- Some embedds them in prosthetics
- It is unsafe for those with signature runes to use their blood on these, as it would cause rune conflicts and cause unexpected behaviours.

# Organic Eidolith
- Beings with signature runes form a core in their body, which can be extracted and used like any other core.
	- Must be fully in tact to be used
- If their will and desire is still strong enough, another core will form over time.
- Eidolith grow with the complexity and quantity of signature runes.
	- People whose core grew so large that it penetrates their body become a lord.
- Some lords' bodies contain more core than flesh. In these cases rune infused prosthetics are installed on them to both suppor their body and to slow down core growth.
- It is important to note that simply using runes more frequenty will only cause Rhyze shells to grow in the user's body. To grow the core, one must obtain more signature runes and derive more rune abilities, or increase the complexity of signature runes.
- One could also go through sugery to mark runes in Rhyze shells, turning them into Eidolith as well. However, these might cause conflicts with the core runes, or evolve signature runes that cause conflicts.
