#concept 
Red crystalline material that grows in [[Nirvana]]'s caves. It is processed into [[Catalysts]] to be used in combination with a [[Driver]] to activate [[Runes#Rune programs]].

- Rhizomic structure
- Rhyze contains sporatic runes
- Unsafe to be used directly
	- Conflicting or destructive runes
- Must be processed into [[Catalysts]] first
- People with no [[Runes]] can activate the effects of runes marked in Rhyze, but since it is difficult to deduce what those runes are it is considered unsafe to do this.

# Rhyze shells
- Repetitive usage of runes cause Rhyze shells to grow at random places of the user's body.
- Can be broken off and harvested as regular Rhyze
# The greatest lie
- People of [[Zinariya]] are taught that Rhyze only grows in Nirvana
- This is a lie. [[Rhyze]] is found everywhere beneath the Earth.
	- This is to limit the Nobles' powers.
	- Places artificial value on Rhyze.
- It grows from the heart of the world outwards in a Rhizomatic structure, and towards [[Welt of the Well]]
