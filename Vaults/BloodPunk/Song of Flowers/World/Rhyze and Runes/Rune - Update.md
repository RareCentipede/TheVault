# Words written in blood
- Characters, symbols created by [[Sangnuis]] that carry Eidolon effects
- Produced by Sangnuis Eidomancers. Symbols they produced when deep in emotions. These symbols then possess parts of their Eidolon.
- These symbols can be redrawn by anyone else and carry those effects.
	- Dropping blood onto these symbols can activate them
- Understanding the symbols and their specific effects require intense analysis. Thus, House Sangnuis has a lot of scholars, even accepting capable commoners into their branch.
