# Overview
- Runes run through all living being's blood
- It is thought to be the language of gods and demons, as it cannot be fully comprehended by humans.
	- The rules seem to be sporatic and inconsistent.
	- In reality, runes describe feeling and emotions.
		- Something humans know best yet cannot fully understand.
- The level of concentration varies from body to body.
	- Runes align to the hosts' emotions and desires
	- High rune concentration and strong emotions/desires are more likely to form signature runes
- Also possible for a valid signature rune to be formed completely by chance, but this extremely unlikely

# Signature runes and rune abilities
## Signatures
- Rune ability templates. Usually inheritated.
- Each of the three [[Noble houses]] has their own signature runes, which can be general characterized as follow:
	- Aphelion: Fire, light (hidden)
	- Kendra: Water, ice
	- Sangnuis: Blood
- However, not every noble has high enough rune concentration to possess a signature rune
	- Noblility children are tested for their rune concentration during the [[Blood Trial]]. Those who fail cannot become fully fledged nobles.
	- They either work as servants or are thrown to Nirvana.
- Branches of signatures exist within each house.
## Abilities
- Rune abilities are derived from signature runes which manipulates the physical world when triggered.
- Rune abilities are consciously defined by the user.
	- For instance, an Aphelion may possess a `Blast` rune signature, which is a branch of `Fire`, and they may manifest a `point blast` ability, or a `blast jump` ability.
		- The former is a long range, high-precision attack, while the second is a close range utility ability, causing high impact but little damage.
	- Weaker nobles might only be able to manifest a limited set of rune abilities.
- Descendents of long bloodliens can manifest rune abilities easier
	- After generations of using a similar set of rune abilities, they are marked deeply in the blood.
- Using rune abilities chip away the user's soul, reflected in the user's flesh being eaten away.
	- The soul repairs itself overtime, and the flesh heals too. Unless the soul is gone completely.
	- Continuous usage is still harmful, and can result in permanent physical damage.
		- Flesh that cannot heal are replaced with rune-infused metal. So the metal corrodes away instead of the flesh and can be replaced.
