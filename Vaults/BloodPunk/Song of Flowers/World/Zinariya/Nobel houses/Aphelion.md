#faction 
# Theme
- Industrial, steampunk
- Heat can power lots of things
- Aphelion means the point in the orbit of a planet, comet, or other body most distant from the Sun.
	- Striving for light and sun but is the farthest from it

# History
- Apollo split the sun into 9 parts. During Ragnarok, all of them wanted to come out and fight Welt and set the world ablaze.
- The last and youngest sun disagreed with his brothers' plan. If they all come out the whole world would be destroyed.
- Before the battle he sought out a human warrior, Solaire, to help him defeat his brothers.
	- He did not go to Welt in case he just kills him right away.
- The 9th sun gave parts of his power to Solaire. Together they killed the other 8 suns one by one.
- After the war, only the 9th sun was left to keep the world lit.
- However, Aphelion was hungry on the power he obtained by slaying the other 8 suns, and attempted to kill the last sun to absorb its power too.
- In its last moment, the 9th sun cursed Aphelion's whole bloodline and the world, such that there would be no sunlight without blood and sacrifices from his own kin.
# Fire and Light
- House Aphelion's core Eidolon keeps the sun lit, providing light and safety for the empire
- The light and warmth is being generated using sacrifices to their Mara
- They see themselves as the most important house, and are highly prestigious

# Far away from light
- Even though the house is striving towards sunlight, their practices cannot be darker.
- Two branches exist within the house:
	- Control faction (Veilwardens)
		- "A sun that never sets leaves no shadows for its masters to rule."
		- Not working towards sunlight without sacrifices
			- Keep themsleves necessary
	- Light faction (The Solmire Pact)
		- "The fire must be fed, lest the world be cast into ruin."
		- "To hesitate is to fade. To sacrifice is to shine."
		- Want to find a way to keep the sun up forever without the need for sacrifices
