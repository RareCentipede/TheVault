#faction 
# Overview
- The three houses possess powerful Eidolons that built up the Empire
- Light and warmth, water and trading, blood and reproduction.
- Since every house is essential for the empire, there is also an acute power balance between them
- They all need each other, which also gives them leverage political plays.

# History
- The founders of the houses were people that helped Welt defeat the gods during Ragnarok.
- Solaire Aphelion took down 8 of the 9 suns. He shared power with the last one to keep the world lit.
- Kendra calmed the oceans and its spirits. She left a fraction of the god in the water to turn the tides and summon spirits.
- Sangnuis healed those affected by the calamities and fought the corruptions. He bathed in the gods blood and reconstructed humans to be stronger.
