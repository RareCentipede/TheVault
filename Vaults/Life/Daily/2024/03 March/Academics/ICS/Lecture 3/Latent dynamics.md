# Latent dynamics
- A high dimensional system that behaves as a low dimensional one. Or
- a low-dimensional system, of which high dimensional data is collected

# Slow manifold
- A sub-manifold of the dynamical system's space, which maybe high-dimensional or infinite, that is invarirant w.r.t. the manifold and to which trajectories converge fast-enough
- If a high-dimensional system has a globally attractive slow manifold, then it behave as a low dimensional system _most of the time_
