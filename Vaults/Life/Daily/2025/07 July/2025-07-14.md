# Project idea
## Goldfish game experience
- User uses controller to scoop up fish
- Fish and random things float around in the pool
- Collision with them triggers haptic feedback
	- Small impact, infrequent
- Dipping hand into the water triggers cooling component
	- This is probably hard to get + not compact

## Baseball game
- Embody oneself onto a baseball player
- Feel the running and catching the ball
	- Vibrations on legs and hand
- Challenge is syncing up the visuals with the actuators
	- The feeling of "I can do it"
- Might make people more depressed
- Maybe limit to swinging and catching
- Can train general motor skills for rehabilitation
	- Hand-eye coordination

## Baseball game 2
- Haptic device for the visually impaired
- Choose the player to follow
- Feel the running and swinging/catching
