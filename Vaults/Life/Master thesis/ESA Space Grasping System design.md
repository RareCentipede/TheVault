## Hardware

## Software requirement
- Keyboard-based teleoperation of the robotic arm and gripper.
- Hand-VICON calibration (transformation matrix between end-effector and VICON object rigidly attached to the arm)
- Closed-loop control using VICON telemetry.
- Compliant control mode.
- Gravity cancellation control mode.
