# Premise
Turn after turn, the sand flows through the hour glass, and turn after turn the living dies, and the dead comes back to living. An eternal cycle. A world robbed of departures and true death. Until, the time keeper was killed and the hour glass stolen. Time shall flow until it runs out. The absolute authority of death terrified even the gods. They abandoned their thrones and erected a tower that soared into the cosmos, escaping death's grasp. But decay is inevitable. Climbing high into the tower only slows down the flow of sand, never stopping it.

Climb the tower. Find the hour glass, and restore our eternity.

# The one at the top
Sipping tea and reading on an ornate chair and desk, on an endless foggy glass plane underneath a brilliantly filled sky of stars and galaxies, and a spiralling void far in the distance.

"A breath-taking sight, is it not? Even after millennia, I still have not gotten tired of it."

"You know, it's quite an annoyance to have your sky shut completely every couple thousand years. Imagine you were enjoying the most amazing brew of tea, and suddenly, someone swats it off the table, spoiling a fine afternoon. So, I set out to stop that. If the damned time keeper stops turning that hour glass, I can enjoy my tea and sky for as long as I want."

"It's quite a curious world. It really is. Before I sat here for 2000 years I was on a long walk. Back that way, if you walk 300 years that void there would only be a speck in the sky, and you'll find a train station. Unfortunately, the next one won't arrive for a very long time, so I didn't wait. I was more curious about this void in the sky."

"No, I haven't started making my way into it yet. I mean, there's no rush, is there? I've got all the time in the world."

"Oh please, don't think so highly of yourselves. You are *nothing* to me."
