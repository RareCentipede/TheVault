Garnt's mushroom crunch story
- "I really hated mushrooms as a kid. The taste is actually pretty good but I just could not stand the crunch. It felt...so gross. Years later, I was eating at my mother's again and she made mushroom soup, and I loved it. The crunch, the taste, the crunchiness, all of it. Sometimes, you have to enjoy the crunch."
	- Loosely transcribed from my memory

This can relate to a lot of things, such as video editing. We enjoy the end product much more than the editing process, which makes it difficult to start or finish a project. But if you grow to enjoy that tedious process, you won't be able to stop making videos. You have to enjoy the process.

The crunch is a process to eating the mushroom. Even though you might like mushroom's taste, the crunch might get in the way. So make yourself enjoy that crunch, find things to like about it. Then all of the mushroom will be a delight to eat and you can't stop wanting more.
