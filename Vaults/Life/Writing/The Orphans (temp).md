A 20 years old boy was murdered in a heavy rain. 10 years later, he opened his eyes and found himself inside the body of a baby. Reincarnated, and given a chance at life again, and on the other side of the world where he was living originally. Eager to find his old friends, family, and the truth about his murder, he put himself through training to gain sufficient skills and money. However, the reincarnation was not simply a gift...

# Settings
- An institution found a way to capture the fleeting soul of a dying person, and through rituals send it to a child that is about to be born.
	- When is a `soul` formed?
	- Is the original `soul` killed to be replaced by the new one?
	- What's the cost of the ritual?
- They started offering a rebirth service to people, in exchange of harvest the organs of their original body.
- The dying MC was tested on, but his soul only found a host 10 years later.
- While in elementary school, he found a girl who was also reincarnated.
- Once the ritual and technology was fully developed, the institution started offering its services to government officials. Anyone willing to pay can be reincarnated. Corrupt politicians, government officials, and monsters can get off the hook and stay hidden, and wait to commit atrocities again.
	- The more you pay, the better the family you will be born into.
- One such monster was reborn near the MC, and it is up to him to stop the monster before he can commit big atrocities, and shutdown the sinister rituals.
