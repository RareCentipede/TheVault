# Prologue: Show Me
A man deemed a heretic by the village burned violently on a stake under the bright moon. The wood cracked and the man cried in the endless night as the crowd watched. A young girl was held back by several men, crying, kicking her legs, begging them to stop the fire.

"It exists! The land of sunlight! A whole kingdom lit by a single torch, and golden crops waving in the wind to feed people rather than the flesh of our own! You have to believe me! It exists, and much more is out there!" The man cried out even as his skin melted. Yu Lan watched behind a row of adults. She could not see the heretic clearly but his words rang in her head. A land of sunlight. She wondered what that must be like. A gigantic torch in the sky? Or a ball of fire? Won't the people get burned just like the heretic? And won't they be blinded by all that light? Her eyes sparkled as the stake cracked. Her whole life had been in the valley, and she could not imagine what the world beyond those storming mountains must be like.

"Desperate bellows of a madman." The priest said and turned to the crowd. "Do not listen to his heresy, my good believers. The world beyond those mountains is rotten and wretched! We must serve our one true lord Vorax and be blessed with his bountiful produce! Heretics like him that try to steer you away are trying to starve us all!" He pointed at the burning heretic. The crowd chanted at his demise and spat at him. Yu Lan pushed through to take a closer look, as she pushed to the front the girl that was held down broke free and ran towards the fire.

"Put out the fire, please! My father will die!" She yelled out as a splinter of wood cracked and flew to her face, scorching the left side of her face and her eye. She screamed and squirmed on the ground. The men rushed over and held her down once more. "Xuan...Don't be afraid. Wherever you go, whatever you choose, I'll be there with you, and guide you through it all. But you mustn't forget about the world that exists outside. Don't be caged in this valley like the rest of us." The fire had scorched his throat, his voice raspy and painful, yet his words gentle.

Yu Lan watched the girl cry and struggle in front of her burning father. Soon enough, he went silent. Only the cracking of the wood and dancing fire remain in the quiet, eternal night. "My believers, you heard the heretic, he wanted to make his *own* daughter into a madman like himself!" He crouched down and gently caressed her burn wound. Fire danced in her eyes, her brows tense as if she could not feel the pain from the burn. "She's just a poor girl, trying to be a good fodder for our lord like the rest of us." He said and sprung up. "We must not let his legacy go on! Find his scriptures and burn them all!" As soon as he finished the crowd dispersed to the man's home and the girl cried out in objection.

Yu Lan ran after the crowd but her mother pulled her away. "Let the adults handle it! It's dangerous, I don't want you to get exposed to those mad writings." She complained but complied. Not long after several men ran back with bundles of books and scrolls in their arms. As they ran passed Lan one of them stumbled and dropped several scriptures. She quickly bent down to pick them up for him, and swiftly hid a few under her clothes and gave the rest back to the man. He gave her thanks and ran to the fire and tossed them all into the flames.

Eventually, only Xuan was left staring at the flames, and the charred remains of her father. Lan watched her as her mother dragged her back home. She wondered how the girl must have felt in that moment, or what she was thinking. Back home, Lan dashed into her room and flipped through the books she had snatched. Each page filled with descriptions of places and things she had never heard of. The path of light, a church devoting itself to the light and helping travellers navigate the night; the cocoon of dreams, a monstrous cocoon holding countless people inside and subjecting them to an eternal sweet dream; the accursed instruments, they played the most beautiful music at the cost of the performer's life; the dandelions, a faction of homunculus all spawned from one host; the pit of fertility, a pit that spawned creatures with no end; and lots and lots more. Lan tirelessly read through each page, her heart thumped with excitement, she had never felt this. The world the books described is so varied and exciting. On the final page was an illustration of a bright yellow circle above an opulent kingdom. The title of this chapter was "Zinariya: the land of sunlight." Lan's hands caressed the drawing, her eyes bright and she felt warm inside, as if something was going to burst out of her at any moment. She looked outside, it is pitch black save for a few torches in the distance, and far in the distance sparks zipped in and out of existence. The same scenery she had been looking for as long as she could remember. The unending lighting storms on the mountains surrounding the valley had everyone trapped, trying to climb over means certain death. With her hopes of seeing these wonder places in vein, Lan blew out the lamps and collapsed among the scattered books. The strange and beautiful sceneries swirled in her head as she imagined herself seeing them with her own eyes. She thought about them again and again, until she lost conscious and fell asleep.

# Act I: Touch Me
The devourance festival was held once again. It is the day the lord gives the people abundance of food and resources. Torches are lit, everyone dressed in bright red hooded robes and dresses, on the hoods are jagged white frails, as if they were being eaten by a great beast. As the town folks danced around the torches, teeth-like pendants hanging around their necks and waist tingled crisply, adding to the music being played by the musicians and singers. The town square was bustling with life. In the middle of crimson hoods stood a sole white petal, receiving the final preparations from the nuns before being fed to the lord. Lan watched in the distance as the nuns rubbed all sorts of creams and paint over his body. Only subjects with impeccable beauty was worthy to be consumed by their lord.

"You look beautiful, Xin." Lan said after the nuns finished their work.
"Lan! Thank you. I'm not sure I can get used to the paint, though." Xin said, his face carrying a feminine beauty with a masculine edge, and strokes of red paint ran all over his face and body, they emanate a soft, sweet scent. "Good thing I won't have to bare it for long," he said and chuckled softly.
"I'm happy for you, really. Not everyone is worthy of being the lord's fodder." Lan said looking to the side at the dancing crowds, Xin's morbid joke made her feel a bit sad.
"You will surely be worthy too! You are also really beautiful, and your poems are sublime."
"Thank you, Xin." Lan said softly, as if something was stuck in her throat. She looked up at his shirtless body, his toned muscles gleamed under the torches and moonlight, she suddenly felt a bit warm and fuzzy.
Xin gently gazed at the fluttering girl in front of him. "Lan, could you...take a piece of me?"
"Oh! Yes, of course." She said. Her eyes traced the red strokes that ran around his body, it ran from his chest to around his arms, then back and down to his abdomen. Her eyes followed the lines lower and lower down to his crotch, the realization of what she was staring at flustered her and she jolted her gaze back up, and met his eyes. "H-how about your neck?" She said and pointed at the side of his neck.
"My neck? Sure," he said and crouched onto the ground. Lan approached Xin, now they are about the same height, she placed her hands on his shoulders, careful to not touch the red pigments. She gently suckled on the pigment on the left side of Xin's neck, nibbling away some of the red lines. She then ran her tongue across his skin and licked away any remaining pigment, and swallowed the paint. The nuns had always made the paint from fruits and pigments, giving it a sweet taste. Lan tasted some saltiness from Xin's sweat. She didn't mind it.
"Thank you, Lan. I will continuing living as a part of you." He said as Lan shyly backed away.
"Xin!" A group of young boys and girls said as they ran towards him. "Congratulations!"
"Thank you guys!"
"Can we take a piece of you as well? We want to be as beautiful and strong as you are, and be the lord's fodder too!"
"Of course, please help yourselves." As soon as he finished the youngsters ran up to him, each one suckling on a different part of his body, eating away the red paint. With their teeth-like red hoodies it looked as if Xin was being eaten alive by numerous hungry monsters.
"Thank you, Xin! We won't forget you!" They said with laughter after they finished. Their mouths turned bright red and their teeth stained red too, as if they just feasted on raw meat. Lan had seen this sight many, many times. But for some reason, this time she felt uncomfortable, so much so that she felt sick and wanted to barf. She ran away to a distant bush away from the crowd and gagged painfully.

When she came to the cermony had officially begun. Xin was being escorted to the lord's chambers, the crowd chanted fiercely.
"Xin!" She yelled out. But he was already at the gate of the chamber. He turned around one last time and met her eyes. He smiled cheerfully and went inside. Lan's heart throbbed, but the crowd cheered and celebrated.
A while later, nuns and priests came out of the chamber with trays piled with food and produce, the crowd cheered and chanted even livelier. Lan pushed through the crowd to a large table where all the gifts from the lord have been stashed. Various meats, both cooked and raw, mountains of vegetables and fruits, and lots of other useful produce such as animal fat, cloth, and chunks of wood were stacked to a hill. Xin's life was exchanged for all these gifts.
The crowd picked at the various foods and began feasting. In the past Lan would join with no hestitation, but this time she had no appetite. Staring at the food and the hungry crowd, she thought about the kingdom the heretic talked about while being burnt to a stake, about how it bathed under the sun and its people did not rely on sacrificing each other for food. Could Xin's fate been avoided? Could he still be here, next to her? Thinking about this, tears rolled down her face. Xin was gone. Forever. His flesh turned into the foods everyone was feasting on.

"My dearest followers," the priest yelled out on a pedestal, "thanks to Xin's diligent efforts, the lord has blessed us with yet another flourishing and abundant month! Let us enjoy this feast in his stead, and give him out most sincere gratitude." He placed both of his hands in front of his chest, closed his eyes and bowed towards the lord's chamber. Everyone in the square followed, Lan was busy wiping her tears and fell slightly behind. A few moments later, the priest turned back to the crowd and began talking again. "I will now announce the gifted children for next year..." the priest began announcing names, each time met with gasps and excitement in the crowd, the chosen was surpised and the ones around them cheered and congratulated them. Lan was not paying attention, her mind filled with thoughts of the kingdom of sun and other brilliant places outside of the valley.

"Yu Lan!"
"Yes!" Lan startled and said.
The crowd erupted, many came up to her to give their congratulations. Lan was dumbfounded, she looked around like a lost child and her eyes met the priest's. A wide and kind smile was on his face. That was when she realized, in a year's time, she too would be devoured by their lord and be turned into fodder for her people.

...

Lan treaded under the eternal moonlight across the plain. The past few weeks had turned life upside down; she got put into a tight schedule of literature and art studies to improve her temperament, and physical trainings with strict diet control to maintain a slender and beautiful body, eating perfectly balanced meals without too much spice or fat. This was to build a beautiful mind and body. A caretaker was also assigned to her to watch her diet and assist her in trainings, and do most mundane daily tasks for her.

Lan was starting to feel miserable, her only escape was when the caretaker went to bed so she could sneak out to read the heretic's books. Even now, even after having read them for perhaps hundreds of times, every page still fascinated her, and she dreamed of going to these places to see them for herself.

Eventually she reached the shore. The only opening in valley. She stared out to the vast, endless sea. The waves came and broke down at the sand, splashing salty water and foam onto Lan. She wondered what would be on the other side, her books did not have descriptions of it. Lan sat on the sand and watched the waves. The moon ever so still and its relfection glittering on the water. She was lost in thoughts when a dark figure in a dinhgy with a long row appeared out of the rocky mountains. It saw Lan and rowed towards the shore. As the boat approached the shore Lan could see it was an old man in a dark robe. She had never met a stranger from outside of her village so she was taken aback and kept her distance.

The man stepped out of the boat and reached out his hand. "Greetings, young girl. I see you long for the sea, I can see the sparkles in your eyes when you stared into the horizon. The sea is a beautiful place, I can take you to it, and many other wonder places your heart desires." His voice raspy with a strange distortion, it was uncomfortable to listen to.

He made Lan feel uneasy, but the thought of finally going out of the valley and seeing the world made her stay. She looked into his eyes, almost hollow, his teeth grossly dark yellow, then down to his extended hand, boney with loose skin and long nails. Yet, she was not repelled. Her head felt light and her chest warm. She can sail on the sea! Perhaps he could even take her to the kingdom that baths in sunlight. Lan reached out her hand to take his.

"Don't listen to that thing!" A voice cried from behind. It was Lan's caretaker. A young girl with a scar on her right eye. She was breathless having ran all the way here. "He's just trying to take you away!"

The girl's scream knocked Lan awake, and suddenly the man felt repulsive and she quickly drew her distance, but not quick enough to avoid his sudden grasp. His boney hand strongly gripped Lan's arm and dragged her towards the boat, she struggled but to no avail. As he dragged her on the sand, kicking up piles of sand into the air, the girl sprinted towards them and smashed his arm with a large rock, throwing Lan onto the sand. The man stumbled and the girl kicked him in the chest, pushing him onto the ground and smashing the rock into his head, splitting it open and spilling out black smoke.

"Quick, get up and run!" The girl said and reached her hand to Lan, it was tainted with black good and smoke. Lan reached out to grab it but black vines suddenly warpped around the girl's body and snatched her away. The man had risen, his half split head twitching and his body grew to an enormous dark shadow, almost shapless and shifting in the wind. Its grip tightened around the girl and she screeched out in pain. Lan sat on the sand trembling in fear, with a hint of excitement at seeing this nightmarish monster, something that broke the the monotonous scenery she had been seeing everyday since she was born, and a sensation that shook her whole body. Questions flooded her mind, what does this shadow man feel like? How was he seeing with a head split open? Where will he take them on his boat? Perhaps the other end of the ocean?

Her trail of exiciting questions was cut short when the monster warpped its grotesque black vines around Lan too. With both of them in its hand it turned and slowly waddled towards the boat.

"Hey! Where is it taking us?" Lan yelled out to the girl.
"Nowhere good!" She said while struggling in its grasp, and eventually managed to stick her left arm out from between its vines. "Lady Lan, could you please trust me?"
"What? Yes, I trust you!"
"Bite my finger with the ring, real hard. Hard enough to make me bleed!"
Lan stretched out her neck to barely reach the girl's hand, "are you sure?"
"Yes, don't worry about me, just bite until I bleed!"
She opened her mouth and bit into the girl's finger. She clenched her teeth and tried not to scream. Lan closed her jaw harder and harder, eventually she tasted the rusty liquid in her mouth and let go.
"This will do it!" Blood flowed down her finger, as soon as it touched the ring it glowed a bright red color under the white moonlight. "Go to hell you piece of shit!" She pointed the ring at the monster and an unrelenting force instantly tore it apart and dug a deep hole on the ground behind the monster. The blast sent them both flying onto the sand. The shadow monster with a gaping hole in its chest stumbled forward and fell onto the sand, and crumbled apart. Its ashes flew away in the wind.

Black vines still wrapped around Lan's body but she easily brushed them off, they crumbled apart like dried leaves. She staggeringly pushed herself up from the sand, she felt pain around her chest like never before, and her white robes now torn apart and tained with dark ashes. Lan dragged herself towards her caretaker, she laid blissfully on the sand, the vines still sticking to her clothes.

"Are you okay?" Lan swatted away the black vines and said.
"Alive," she said and examined her bleeding finger and bloody ring, it had lost the sheen.
"What was that? How did you kill that monster?"
"It's an Eidolith. It contains an ability that can be activated with blood."
"Where did you get it? Do you have more? Are there others with different abilities?" She grabbed her hand and asked, the stone had lots of small marking on the inside.
"Nevermind that," she said and pulled away her hand, "what were you doing all the way out here, lady Lan? You are an important fodder for the lord and you almost got yourself killed!"
"I...came here to read...the ocean helps me relax." She said and got up. "Oh! My book!" Lan frantically searched around and spotted it near where she first fell on the sand. "Thank goodness I didn't lose it."

Lan flipped through the book to check if the contents got damage and did not notice the looming shadow from behind.
"Lady Lan, where did you get this book?"
"Huh? Oh! It's...I found it on my father's shelf..."
The girl tried to snatch the book but Lan had a strong grip on it.
"What are you doing? Stop!"
"This book must be destroyed! It's heresy!"
"Let it go!" Lan kicked the girl in the chest and her hand finally slipped, knocking her back onto the sand. She grimaced in pain.
"Sorry! Are you okay?"
"I'm fine...But you must give me that book. It's dangerous," she said, her eyes twinkled in in the moonlight, its shine lit up her face and clearly displayed the scar on her left eye.
Lan bend down and stared at her face, she thought the girl looked familiar but never figured out from where. The scar looked the same as the one on the girl whose father burnt to stake in front of her.

The girl looked away.
"He's your father, wasn't he? The author of this book."
"..." She nodded.
"Then your name is-"
"Xuan. Zhi Xuan. Daughter of the accursed heretic."
