# The valley of hunger
- Surrounded by high mountains of unrelenting storm. They were either too high to climb or the top suffered constant lightning strikes.
- Far down south is a large opening. Between the two cliffs sat a lonely beach that opened to the sea.
	- However, no one in the village had any ship building knowledge or skills. And wood was too scarce to be used on experimental boats.
	- Over time, ideas of leaving and sailing the sea became forbidden and taboo.
- There were a couple of woods, their fruits inedible.
- A special type of silkworm grew in the valley. Their silk produced a faint light, they also make patterns on their cocoons to scare away prey.
- Lots of fireflies, the main source of light for the villagers. Their eyes adapted to the dark, they do not require too much light to see properly, even for reading.
- The lightning often cracking the rocks on top of the mountains, imbuing them with power.
	- The power stones were smelted down to make efficient tools.
- The villagers slept a lot to conserve energy.

# Devourance
- In the valley, the only way to obtain food is by sacrificing youngsters to the lord.
- He will eat them, and provide abundance of produce for the people.
- The festival took place once a month. A big celebration to send off the sacrifice and to receive fresh produce.
- Everyone had accepted this way of life.
