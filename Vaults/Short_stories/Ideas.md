# [[The empty throne]]
The protagonist defeats all his foes and took over the kingdom. He longed for the glory and honour. However,  celebration and novelty of his new position quickly waned off. And it all began to feel empty.
OR
The hero slayed the tyrannical God and brought peace to his realm. But something did not feel quite right. The corrupt God died too easily. It all ended so smoothly. But all is well now so he did not think too much of it. The celebration went on for days, and the hero's consciousness began to fade. His body crumbled bit by bit.
